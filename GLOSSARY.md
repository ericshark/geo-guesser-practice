# GeoGuessr Academy

A personal, local-only learning app for recognising GeoGuessr clues. A curriculum of topics and lessons teaches clues; practice and review keep them.

## Curriculum

**Topic**:
A kind of clue taught as a unit (Foundations, Scripts & languages, Text clues, and the roadmap: road lines, signs, bollards, plates, poles, car & camera, landscape, architecture, big-country regions). Topics have an order that follows GeoGuessr value.
_Avoid_: Section, category, module

**Lesson**:
A short set of at most six clues from one topic, studied then practised in one session.
_Avoid_: Level, unit

**Clue**:
The atomic thing to know and the unit that is scheduled and mastered, e.g. "Portuguese", "Kenya drives on the left", ".ch web addresses". A clue has a one-line **spot** (what to look for), the countries it applies to, and the look-alikes it is confused with.
_Avoid_: Skill, card, item

**Stimulus**:
What a question shows for a clue (a sign, a sentence, a web address, a price). A clue has several stimuli and a different one is shown each time, so learners recognise the clue rather than memorise a prompt.
_Avoid_: Sample (used only for the raw language data), prompt

**Tell**:
A specific feature inside a stimulus that points to a clue (e.g. `ß` → German, `ő` → Hungarian). Highlighted after answering.

**Confusable / Foil**:
A **confusable** is another clue that is easily mistaken for this one, with a note on how to tell them apart. A **foil** is a wrong answer that is not itself a clue (e.g. a right-hand-traffic country in a "which drives on the left?" question).

## Learning and progress

**Session**:
One run of questions: a lesson, a review of due clues, mixed practice (all learned clues or one region), a mix-up drill, or a country drill. All sessions use the same player.

**Mastery**:
Derived from FSRS memory stability: **New**, **Learning**, **Familiar** (≥ 4 days), **Mastered** (≥ 21 days). Mastery cannot be reached in one sitting.

**Due**:
A clue whose FSRS review time has passed. Due clues are reviewed first and are folded into lessons.

**Mix-up**:
A repeated clue-to-clue confusion, offered as a short drill on the dashboard.

## Geography

**Playable Country**:
A country or distinct GeoGuessr territory (e.g. Hong Kong, Puerto Rico, Faroe Islands) with one shared record of coverage tier, driving side, sign languages, domain and currency.

**Coverage Tier**:
**Official** (broad road Street View), **Limited** (partial, select cities or trekker-only) or **None**.
